## Civilian team/City improvements
Making the city interactive would be such a cool detail in the game and here's my plan how it can be done. Anomic is a game on roblox that demonstrates alot of these ideas.
- Civilians can purchase buildings in the city.
- Civilians that purchase a building can build on it, wether it be a shop, house, restaurant, armory, you name it.
- Add a hospital to the city (Spawn for [Paramedics](/pages/paramedic.md))
- Let civilians sell items like food, maybe cars, basic weapons, a knock-off bloxy that can play a part in plant riots, etc...
  - This selling mechanic could make a player-based economy in the game
- give civilians more vehicle options (Wagons, SUVs, Jeeps, Hatchbacks, Trucks, Maybe an improvised armored car)
