## REVAMP THE EMERGENCY LIGHTING SYSTEM
Current Els is super buggy when it doesn't have to be that way. It's extremely simple too in a cheap way. A good ELS doesn't have to be overengineered but also doesn't have to be made in 5 minutes either. [ELRC](https://www.roblox.com/games/2534724415/Emergency-Response-Liberty-County) and [Greenville](https://www.roblox.com/games/891852901/Greenville) on Roblox are games that demonstrate a great ELS system. It does not have to be complex but it atleast has to deliver great quality.
## characteristics of the new emergency lighting system
- It will have 4 lighting modes
  1. > Cruise mode has the some of the lightbar steady on and not flashing to show presence.
  ![ ](/staticContent/cruisemode.webp)
  2. > Scene mode (code 1) flashes the headlights, tail lights, and some of the lightbar at a constant pace for the purpose of being calm at scene.
  ![ ](/staticContent/code1.webp)
  3. > Normal mode (code 2) flashes the headlights, tail lights, and the whole lightbar at a normal pace thats sterotypical for cop cars.
  ![ ](/staticContent/code2.webp)
  4. > Chase mode (code 3) flashes the headlights, tail lights, and the whole lightbar fast and agressively to grab as much attention as possible.
  ![ ](/staticContent/code3.webp)
- It will have other features like:
  - More realistic siren behavior.
  - A "manual" button that when pressed will mute the current siren to play the siren wail until the button is let go of then unmute the current siren again.
  - > Controls for moving and toggling spotlight (Demo for pointing spotlight below)
  ![ ](/staticContent/spotlightControlDemo.webp)
  - Controls for toggling lightbar takedown lights
