## REVAMP THE EMERGENCY LIGHTING SYSTEM
Current Els is super buggy when it doesn't have to be that way. It's extremely simple too in a cheap way. A good ELS doesn't have to be overengineered but also doesn't have to be made in 5 minutes either.
## characteristics of the new emergency lighting system
- It will have 4 lighting modes
  1. > Cruise mode has the some of the lightbar steady on and not flashing to show presence.
  ![ ](/staticContent/cruiseMode.gif)
  2. Scene mode flashes the headlights, tail lights, and some of the lightbar at a slow pace for the purpose of being calm at scene.
  3. Normal mode flashes the headlights, tail lights, and the whole lightbar at a normal pace thats sterotypical for cop cars (Known as code 2)
  4. Chase mode flashes the headlights, tail lights, and the whole lightbar fast and agressively to grab as much attention as possible (Known as code 3)
