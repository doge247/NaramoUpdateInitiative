## ADD A BANK INTO NARAMO CITY
ALOT OF THE BANK GOT INSPIRATION FROM THE DEPOSITORY FOUND IN ANOMIC ON ROBLOX!
Adding a bank to naramo could easily add a new challenge to naramo, and the best part is that you dont need a new team to run a bank. At bare minimum, the only staff that the bank needs are [Logistical Operations](https://naramo-nuclear-plant-v2.fandom.com/wiki/Logistical_Operations) and [Local Military Police](https://naramo-nuclear-plant-v2.fandom.com/wiki/Local_Military_Police)
## Whats new for staff
- Logistics in will be responsible of new cargo type: Money
- A regular Armored Car with 1 logistic and 2 security personnel can pick up money from the bank and drop it off in the plant to ensure DoE staff can get bonuses
- As long as the bank has money, staff will get their paycheck. They loose all pay if the bank runs out of money including ["Holding port"](https://naramo-nuclear-plant-v2.fandom.com/wiki/XP#Holding_Ports).
- The bank can run out of money either from a robbery by: 
  - [Externals](https://naramo-nuclear-plant-v2.fandom.com/wiki/Role_Hub#External) robbing the bank,
  - Repeated cash pickup from logistics,
  - Or passively from staff paychecks
- There will be a treasury, either in the [F.O.B MARENT](https://naramo-nuclear-plant-v2.fandom.com/wiki/Locations?#East_Military_Base) or preferably a new place still far from the city
- In order to refill the bank of money, logistics and atleast 4 security personnel will need to drive to the treasury, Spawn the now available Heavy Armored Car and drive it to the city bank to unload the cash bags.
- Unloading cash bags is manual and leaves Logistics personnel vunrable which is why security is needed.
- once the bank is refilled from the Heavy Armored Car, All staff get paid their missing paychecks
- The bank will run out of money faster, the most XP DoE makes
- Once the bank gets refilled, If the total missing paychecks are greater than the bank's balance, The bank will need to be refilled over and over until all the missing paychecks get paid.
- The bank should only need to be refilled around once every 15 minutes in a [Teir 1 shift](https://naramo-nuclear-plant-v2.fandom.com/wiki/XP#End-Of-Shift_Bonus:) server.
## Whats new for Externals
- Civilians could rob the bank but only if the [civilian team gets improved](/pages/civilians.md) first off
- The [WNAF](https://naramo-nuclear-plant-v2.fandom.com/wiki/West_Noobian_Assault_Force), [BFO](https://naramo-nuclear-plant-v2.fandom.com/wiki/Bloxian_Commonwealth), [FRR](https://naramo-nuclear-plant-v2.fandom.com/wiki/Federal_Ranger_Regiment), and [FMDF](https://naramo-nuclear-plant-v2.fandom.com/wiki/Federal_Maritime_Defense_Force) could rob the bank to help disrupt the shift and get some XP for themselves.
- WN could get passive XP whenever the bank is down just like if they were holding the port.
## Vehicles in depth
- Both the Armored Car and Heavy Armored Car will share the same engine compartment as the [Mass Casualty Bus](/pages/paramedic.md#mass-casualty-bus)/[Medium-Duty Ambulance](/pages/paramedic.md#medium-duty-ambulance).
- Both vehicles have the same armor (NOT HEALTH) as the [HTV](https://naramo-nuclear-plant-v2.fandom.com/wiki/HTV).
- If the money truck gets destroyed, It can be robbed for XP
