## ADD A BANK INTO NARAMO CITY
ALOT OF THE BANK GOT INSPIRATION FROM THE DEPOSITORY FOUND IN ANOMIC ON ROBLOX!
Adding a bank to naramo could easily add a new challenge to naramo, and the best part is that you dont need a new team to run a bank. At bare minimum, the only staff that the bank needs are [Logistical Operations](https://naramo-nuclear-plant-v2.fandom.com/wiki/Logistical_Operations) and [Local Military Police](https://naramo-nuclear-plant-v2.fandom.com/wiki/Local_Military_Police)

- Logistics in will be responsible of new cargo type: Money
- A regular Armored Car with 1 logistic and 2 security personnel can pick up money from the bank and drop it off in the plant to ensure DoE staff can get bonuses
- As long as the bank has money, staff get a bonus paycheck like keeping the port + their own paycheck + other bonus paychecks. They loose all pay if the bank runs out of money.
- The bank can run out of money either from a robbery by civilians/WN or by repeated cash pickup from logistics
- There will be a treasury, either in the F.O.B MARENT or preferably a new place still far from the city
- In order to refill the bank of money, logistics and some security personnel will need to teleport to the treasury, Spawn the now available Heavy 18-Wheel Armored Car
