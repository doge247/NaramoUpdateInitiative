## ADD A BANK INTO NARAMO CITY
Adding a bank into the city can integrate into already existing jobs like logistics, civilians, military police, west noobian defense force, or even make new jobs dedicated to the bank.
- Logistics in responsible of new cargo type: Money
A basic Armored Car with armed security personnel can pick up money from the bank and drop it off in the plant to ensure DoE staff can get bonuses
As long as the bank has money, staff get a bonus paycheck like keeping the port + their own paycheck + other bonus paychecks. They loose all pay if the bank runs out of money.
The bank can run out of money either from a robbery by civilians/WN or by repeated cash pickup from logistics
There will be a treasury, either in the F.O.B MARENT or preferably a new place still far from the city
In order to refill the bank of money, logistics and some security personnel will need to teleport to the treasury, Spawn the now available Heavy 18-Wheel Armored Car
