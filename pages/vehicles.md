## Ideas for vehicle system
The current vehicle system is so dookie, from inconsistencies in vehicle designs, wheel glitches, not built for the newer mesh vehicles, limited functionality.
- Add a tool to repair vehicles
- Allow player to control vehicles lights, horns, turn signals, and extra auxiliaries.
- Make tail lights, reverse lights, and running lights work in accordance to the vehicle braking, having headlights on, and/or it reversing.
- Allow vehicles to be locked/unlocked for convenience.
- Bullet marks stick on to bulletproof glass and Bullets instantly shatter normal glass.
- Slow down time before vehicles despawn after being destroyed.
- Make new vehicle system account for vehicle weight (Acceleration is messed up since vehicle remodels are heavier)
- Add a recovery vehicle that can tow a destroyed, damaged, or vaccant vehicles to base.
  - Bringing any vehicles back to base despawns it and rewards the player with XP that increases with vehicle value, It doesn't bring much but is still cool.
  - If a destroyed vehicle that has a cooldown[^1] gets towed back to base, The cooldown time gets cut in half.
  - If a damaged or vaccant vehicle with a cooldown gets towed back to base, The cooldown is gone and the vehicle is ready to be spawned again.
  - This new mechanic will encourage teamwork just like [scouting](https://naramo-nuclear-plant-v2.fandom.com/wiki/Binoculars) does. 
- Make the vehicle suspension more functional[^2]
- Allow cars to make sharp turns[^3]
- Make acceleration realistic[^4]
  [^1]: Vehicles with cooldowns are not vehicles with despawn timers, I am referring to vehicle pads that only let you spawn a vehicle once until the timer on the pad ends. A common example of a vehicle with a cooldown would be the Bradely also known as the [XM64A1](https://naramo-nuclear-plant-v2.fandom.com/wiki/XM64A1)
  [^2]: Better mobility and can help vehicles get unstuck
  [^3]: Better mobility and control benefits combatting infantry and during raids, Plus it helps get vehicles unstuck
  [^4]: The slower you go, the lower the gear. The lower the gear, The higher the torque. High torque lets you climb hills instead of stalling
